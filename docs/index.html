Matrix sandbox

A 2×2 matrix is decided by where it sends the two basis vectors. Its first column is where î lands, its second is where ĵ lands. Drag the arrow tips or type numbers. The gold square shows the determinant as an area, and dashed lines mark the eigenvectors.

Span game

Land on the gold ring using only u and v. Pick α and β so that αu + βv hits the target. Watch out for traps: if u and v are parallel, they only span a line, and the target may be out of reach. Every three hits takes you up a level.

Week 9 · eigenvectors

Google’s PageRank

P·r = 1·r

The original ranking of web pages is the eigenvector for eigenvalue 1 of a huge matrix of link probabilities.

Week 6 · inverse matrix

Leontief’s economy

x = (I − A)⁻¹·d

How much each sector must produce to meet final demand d. This input–output model won Wassily Leontief the 1973 Nobel Prize in economics.

Week 7 · quadratic forms

Portfolio risk

σ² = wᵀ·Σ·w

The variance of a portfolio with weights w is a quadratic form in the covariance matrix Σ. It is positive semidefinite, because a variance can’t be negative.

Week 3 · matrix product

Every video game frame

v′ = M·v

Each rotation, zoom and camera move is a matrix multiplication, applied to millions of vertices per frame on the graphics card.
